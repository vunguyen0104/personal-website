## Scope

`index.html` — the whole of vunguyen.io. One scrolling page, zero build step, GitHub Pages.

## Visitor mode

Persuade. The visitor decides whether this person is real and worth contacting. Three audiences on one page: recruiters skimming for 45 seconds, engineering peers arriving from GitHub, and anyone following a resume link with no hiring intent.

## Job and action

Believe "engineer who ships end-to-end, and has for twenty years." Act by reaching out — the contact form or direct email — or by opening a live project.

## Proof and content

Real only: three shipped projects with working URLs, three employers with dated tenures, three degrees, four certificates with two linkable artifacts. Existing project screenshots and the VTN logo. A portrait the user will supply. No testimonials, metrics, or press exist; none may be invented.

## Constraints

Zero-build static HTML/CSS/JS. No npm, no Pages Action. Contact form must keep working (Bootstrap Studio `smartform-handler`, unverified delivery, mailto fallback required). Bootstrap, AOS, Font Awesome and Ionicons are dropped; icons are authored SVG.

## Chosen direction

Transit Authority (seed 7bc7fdd3) — Massachusetts civic signage: enamel station panels, color-coded route bands, timetable rules, tactile yellow edge. Deep navy ink on concrete ground; red / orange / green / blue as functional line roles, one per destination.

## Memorable moment

The Line: work and study rendered as two parallel colored tracks sharing one chronological corridor from 1994 to now, every job and degree a dated station. Selecting Experience or Education traces that line and mutes the other's color without touching text contrast. The track draws itself once on approach; reduced-motion ships it fully drawn.
